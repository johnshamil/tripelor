# Store Screenshot Plan

Capture real app screens after the production candidate is installed. Do not use device frames or show personal booking data.

## Recommended portrait set

1. **Your Maldives Escape** — Home hero and prominent Quick Booking card
2. **Handpicked Island Stays** — Stay cards with full-size property photography
3. **Book in Three Easy Steps** — Trip step with the native date picker visible
4. **Made for Two** — Couple packages and inclusions
5. **Transfers Made Simple** — Speedboat request screen
6. **Plan With Confidence** — Travel information and personal support

## Google Play

- Upload at least four portrait screenshots.
- Recommended capture size: **1080 × 1920 px** (9:16).
- Required feature graphic: **1024 × 500 px**.
- Required high-resolution icon: **512 × 512 px**.

## Apple App Store

- Upload the largest iPhone screenshot size requested by App Store Connect; Apple can scale that set to smaller iPhone display sizes.
- Keep the same six-screen story and use screenshots from the iOS build.
- Tripelor is configured for iPhone only in version 1.0, so iPad screenshots are not required.

## Capture checklist

- Use attractive sample dates that show availability.
- Keep Wi-Fi, time, and battery indicators clean and consistent.
- Never show a real guest's name, email, phone number, or booking request.
- Confirm every caption describes functionality visible in the screenshot.
